# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated: April 25, 2026**

Bookr Enterprise ("we", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our Partners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Bookr Partner mobile application.

### 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect information necessary to provide and improve our services:

- **Account Data:** Business name, owner name, email address, phone number, and KYC/business verification documents.
- **Venue Data:** Static physical addresses of the venues/services you list.
- **Financial Data:** Bank account details or UPI IDs required for processing payouts and commissions.
- **Staff Data:** Information regarding staff members (names, roles) added by you to manage your venue/services.
- **Media Data:** Photos, videos, and documents uploaded by you to showcase your venue, services, or events.
- **Operational Data:** Booking history, service availability, and staff names.
- **Technical Data:** Device information and logs via Sentry for error reporting and application stability.

### 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

- To facilitate and manage bookings between you and Customers.
- To process payouts and manage financial transactions.
- To enable staff management and role-based access for your team.
- To provide analytics and dashboard reporting on your business performance.
- To communicate important updates regarding your account or the platform.
- To identify and fix technical bugs using Sentry.

### 3. DATA SHARING & THIRD PARTIES

We do not sell your data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** We use **Supabase** for secure database management and backend services.
- **Error Reporting:** We use **Sentry** to monitor application performance and crashes.
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by Indian law or government authorities.
- **Customers:** We share necessary venue details (address and name) with Customers to fulfill bookings. We do not share your private phone number with customers for all booking types unless necessary for service fulfillment.

### 4. DATA SECURITY

We implement industry-standard security measures provided by our infrastructure partners (Supabase) to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

### 5. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

- Access and update your profile and venue information through the App.
- Request the deletion of your account and associated data.
- Opt-out of non-essential communications.

### 6. LOCATION DATA

The App does not track your real-time location. We only store the static business addresses provided by you for the purpose of listing your services to Customers.

### 7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy within the App.
